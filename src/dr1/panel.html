Click a lane's name to hear that voice and edit it here. Shift-click plays it without selecting — and records the hit when DR·1 is armed. Verb sends this voice to its own gated reverb and Gate is how long that reverb runs before it is cut — the tail is chopped rather than allowed to ring, which is the whole effect. Every voice has its own, so a huge snare costs the kick nothing. In P-Lock mode these six faders write a parameter lock onto the selected step: a tom that walks down, a hat that opens on the offbeat, a snare that is dry until the fill. Double-click a fader to take its lock off.
