Record starts on the bar line, not when you press it — arm it a beat early and play into the count.

About the loop

The loop is a fixed number of samples, worked out from the tempo when you armed it. Audio cannot stretch, so changing tempo afterwards means it no longer fits the bar — the tempo it was cut at is shown beside the state. Re-record to move it.

Use headphones. An open microphone into speakers records its own output, and an overdub builds that up every pass until it howls.
