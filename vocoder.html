Press Listen to open the modulator. Use headphones — a microphone into speakers will feed back.

Squeeze is what stops the vocoder needing a hot input: a band opens in proportion to its energy, so without compression the whole output level tracks how loudly you speak. Unvoiced is a separate noise path, because a pitched carrier physically cannot produce “s” or “t”.
